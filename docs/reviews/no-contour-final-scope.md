The following tracked reviews explain the API/source/probe reuse and maintained
controls. None claims complete double-box integration.

  genuine positive-projective source and RootBound ownership, native degree-2/32

  endpoint transport remains explicitly unsupported.
- [Recursive endpoint covers](no-deformation-recursive-endpoint-cover.md):

  positive Jacobian composition, without a general termination claim.
- [Retained margin replay](no-deformation-retained-margin-replay.md) and
  [positive scaling](no-deformation-retained-margin-scale.md): source-derived
  polynomial/role/domain association and replay of original certificate bytes;
  a proposed positive rational scale must pass exact equality.
- [Canonical full measure](no-deformation-canonical-measure.md): native rational
  cancellation of the full composed measure with exact cross-product, resource
  and net-order checks; original coordinate/source denominator ancestry remains.
- [Margin discovery](no-deformation-factor-margin-discovery.md): bounded smaller
  positive trial margins, each requiring native proof and replay. An unknown
  certificate search never becomes a successful unit.

These address individual algebraic/proof obstacles. They do not prove that the
remaining generic moving-root atlas is cheap, or that absence of a sufficient
common ε strip would make the underlying physical integral infeasible.
