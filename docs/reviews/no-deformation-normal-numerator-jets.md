# Compact numerator derivatives through selected roots

`NormalNumeratorJets` retains the actual `SourceNumerators`, issued endpoint
chart and normalized selected-root owners. It prepares absolute Taylor
coefficients in one named normal coordinate. Symbolica expands a tagged call
to the original numerator, differentiating its small composed arguments and
selected-root calls before restricting the normal to zero. Only source partial
derivatives demanded by that expansion are registered. The original numerator
is never expanded after substituting the full nonlinear map.

The coefficients are Taylor coefficients, including the native factorial
normalization. They are neither a leading-valuation certificate nor a truncated
replacement for the bulk numerator. An all-zero finite jet cannot establish
that the original numerator is zero. No numerical-dual endpoint differentiation
or separate CAS is introduced.

## Admission and native reuse

Existing normalized-section certificates supply the selected branch. Their
polynomial coefficient maps extend to the closed parameter box; the retained
positive derivative margin remains nonzero there by continuity. Weak opposite
endpoint signs and strict monotonicity give a unique root on each closed
bracket, including roots at bracket endpoints. The implicit function theorem
then gives the required local smooth extension. Actual prefix order and the
issued chart's polynomial closed-cube maps preserve this association. A change
in the polynomial's leading degree is not a failure of its selected nonzero
root derivative.

Finite source arguments have a separate gate. Native rational-polynomial
conversion retains their exact numerator/denominator identity. The denominator
at normal zero must be nonzero on the remaining open directions and closed
root brackets. Exact constants use native Rational arithmetic; other cases use
the existing native domain certificate on slightly enlarged positive root
brackets, retaining the denominator's orientation. A source-coordinate pole in
the selected normal is refused even when another density factor might cancel
it. The previously implemented independent-root infinity path remains separate.

The API/source audit and focused probes exercise native `Atom::series`, tagged
derivative definitions, selected-root callback scope, evaluator composition
and exact evaluator serialization. A genuinely normal-dependent root gives a
nonzero cubic coefficient that disappears if the root is incorrectly frozen
before differentiation. Independent native etale derivative evaluations at
128 and 256 bits agree with the compact coefficients.

## Execution and mapping semantics

Caller function mappings execute only in a source-coefficient stage. Its actual
outputs feed the original-coordinate partial bodies; a separate validation
stage would be insufficient for transitive aliases. Geometry, selected-root
calls and final assembly use protected function maps. For example, mapping
`A` to `x` and `x` to zero cannot silently replace a live source-coordinate
argument inside a partial derivative. Literal numbers remain in the bodies,
preserving native integer-power evaluation at zero.

One final native program composes all requested coefficients and retained
geometry diagnostics. Complex source coefficients remain complex. Native
callback ownership, error tracking and serialization are reused; detached
restoration without the live source/root owner is not claimed. Mutable numeric
scratch remains caller-owned and consumes no sampling RNG streams.

## Validation and limits

Four maintained groups pass: actual original/refined charts, the implicit cubic
chain, complex coefficients, 128/256-bit evaluation, error tracking, live-scope
codec restoration, alias isolation, finite rational denominators, genuine zero
source inputs, and typed domain/pole/resource/cancellation failures. Independent
scientific and ecosystem reviews pass. Native library and test-source strict
lint checks pass; the final change preserving numeric literals is included in
the maintained execution gate.
The consuming workspace/all-target Clippy gate passes with warnings denied
in 35.03 seconds; formatting and diff checks also pass.

These tests do not establish the physical 156-term jet workload, every closed
face, a common analytic-regulator strip or a physical integration kernel.
Remaining direction coordinates stay strictly inside their relative face;
their own boundaries require corresponding admission and regularization.
