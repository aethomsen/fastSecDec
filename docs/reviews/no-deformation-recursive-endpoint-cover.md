The previous constructor delegates to the same implementation. A refinement replaces that one chart by its complete half-cube and native sector inventory; callers retain every sibling outside the replacement.

The implementation reuses FastSecDec's native sector support/decomposition and Symbolica exact polynomial operations. The composed positive Jacobian contains the added half/sector Jacobian exactly once. Source preparation, selected section owners, coefficient maps and artificial midpoint ancestry remain attached through the parent chain. Fresh target roles are checked against every ancestor inventory.
