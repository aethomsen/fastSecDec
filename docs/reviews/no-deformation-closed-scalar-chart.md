# Closed scalar chart: source and native reuse review

`ClosedScalarChart` admits the complete regulator-dependent density on one
genuinely issued endpoint chart. It requires positive closed-box margins for
every signed residual numerator, its denominator, and the residual measure.
It retains the original preparation, factor links, selected root owners,
full monomial powers, causal phases and meromorphic prefactor witness.
It does not issue a complete cell cover or an integral result.

The implementation reuses `IssuedEndpointChart`, the existing native factor
`ProofBox` and replayable symGCAD margin certificates, normalized-section
`RootProgram`/`Scope`, `causal_negative_phase`, `ConvergenceStrip::admit` and
`MeromorphicWitness`. The proof box contains the closed unit cube and the
retained selected-root brackets. Polynomial unit maps preserve the original
coefficient-box containment and nonzero-minor authority by composition.
The larger independent root box is only a sufficient proof domain: a proof
miss does not establish a zero on the selected algebraic branch.

The density uses the full original factor/coordinate exponents and the
transformed measure once. Native phase construction handles negative causal
factors. Coordinate-dependent polynomial numerators are explicitly unsupported
by this scalar class; they are never discarded. Root calls use the actual
composed section parameter images, retain the native scope and reserve all
source, geometry and coefficient roles. Fresh exportable root tags are searched
with a bounded, budget-checked loop.

Two maintained native tests pass. A projective source with a negative causal
factor, nonconstant positive factor and Gamma prefactor checks the full epsilon
body, its regulator derivative, all margin replays and an exact source-density
identity at nonzero epsilon. The second test distinguishes an unresolved
upper-face zero from the opposite orientation that extracts the zero into
the monomial power `-2-epsilon`; the latter retains its genuine initial strip
`Re(epsilon) < -1`. Term-budget exhaustion and cancellation refuse explicitly.

The original compile errors and failed test orientation are retained. That
test initially expected refusal after the native map had already extracted
the zero correctly; only its fixture orientation and positive control changed.
The final two tests passed in 0.03 seconds. Source and test evidence is frozen
under `target/no-deformation-closed-scalar-owner/review-r1/`; combined workspace
checks are recorded separately by the coordinator.

The strip is common to all terms of this chart, not to other charts. A complete
family must intersect every chart's strip, retain every replacement sibling
and original source cell, and pass through the shared symbolic subtraction
and Laurent compiler. The ongoing double-box cell-0 studies are partial
geometry; neither formal absence of zero face polynomials nor successful
certificates on selected descendants establish completion of its entire
644,699-cell catalogue.
