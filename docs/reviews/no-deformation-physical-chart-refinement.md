`ContinuedRefinementFrontier` retries every original ancestor and captured child

but cannot discard the ambient density or another ancestor. The frontier is
caller-stepped, retains pending work on budget exhaustion, and preserves
accepted work on retries. Invalid evidence remains an error. Its recovery is
in memory; durable recovery and aggregate RSS remain separate gates.
