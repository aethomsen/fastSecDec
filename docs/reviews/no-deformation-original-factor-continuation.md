# Original-factor certificates and the next ambient cycle

Status: independently reviewed local G3 implementation, 2026-10-11. This extends
the [physical-chart refinement](no-deformation-physical-chart-refinement.md).
It does not complete the general resolver, the real integration atlas, or the
endpoint/density acceptance contract.

## Scientific boundary

`AmbientCycleCarry` associates an original marked source with its actual first
coefficient-ascent chart and second supported-continuation chart. It checks each
original generator's total pullback against its current controlled transform and
accumulated exceptional factor. Each exceptional power follows the actual birth
and strict-divisor receipt, including absent and unit cases. The full original
coordinate Jacobian follows the same maps.

The original companion is associated through its native constructor, normalized
ideal, mark and geometric owners. Equal polynomial text does not authorize
splicing an independently constructed source, factor inventory, center or map
into that lineage. The current carry requires both maps to descend from the
same original coefficient hierarchy; arbitrary-length and mixed ancestry remain
outside this constructor.

`OriginalTerminalInventory` checks each original polynomial separately. A
principalization certificate for the ideal generated by several polynomials
does not establish normal crossings for their union of zeros. Every original
factor must have a unit remaining ratio and pass its full reconstruction
identity. A nonunit ratio leaves an explicit unresolved record. This is a
sufficient local admission path; it may refuse factors a more general
simultaneous resolver could resolve.

The two-generator control `(f, x*f)` is deliberately adversarial. It is not the
production encoding of a union divisor. That input must use the product of the
inventoried factors, or a proved equivalent simultaneous-resolution contract,
while retaining individual identities and multiplicities. These local objects
inventory source polynomials, not yet physical F/U/domain/root-gap labels and
their complete density semantics.

`AmbientDropInventory` admits a new cycle only after every transported original
companion has empty cosupport and the complete physical component inventory
proves a strictly smaller residual maximum. Pending or zero-ideal components
prevent a complete maximum. An empty intermediate coefficient ideal or a failed
contact guard is insufficient. The new cycle takes its old-divisor snapshot
from the existing history; it does not create a birth or reset chronology.
This transition follows the companion-cycle distinction in
[Bierstone–Milman, §5, Step II.B, pp. 629–630](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/41043):
the companion transform statements apply to its admissible sequence; after a
residual drop the construction starts a new companion. Extending this local
implementation must retain retired companions as proof ancestry rather than
assume they remain admissible for every later physical map.

`AmbientContinuationCenter` checks that new cycle, its companion and the actual
source-open restriction. `AmbientNextChart` checks every original generator and
the complete composed Jacobian through the third physical map. Its retained
upper cover concerns marked cosupport. It is not a certificate that those opens
alone cover the integration domain.
