# Retained power presentations through subsequent physical maps

Status: independently reviewed local resolver implementation. This extends the
[initial power adapter](no-deformation-principal-power-recursion.md); it does
not complete the general resolver or a real integration atlas.

`CarriedPowerState` consumes an actual original-tree slot and its first-map
receipt. It retains the original, normalized and effective marked ideals,
the unit, supported frame and history. Subsequent steps use an already-issued
physical map. They cannot factor a transformed polynomial afresh or reset the
source's construction history.

For `p = u*g^k`, the existing native operations check original and effective
center admissibility, controlled transforms, ordinary unit pullback, and both
total and controlled identities. The original localization supplies unit
authority. A positive expression alone is insufficient: the maintained
`1+x^2` control refuses to treat positivity as an issued algebraic inverse.

Three outcomes remain distinct. The marked source can have empty cosupport;
its maximum-order companion can have empty cosupport; or a physical chart can
have empty strict lower support. Native cosupport proofs check both original
and effective mates. `SourceCompleted` keeps the source at its last owning
frame. A completed companion also stays at that frame while the source may
continue. Neither authorizes a later controlled division, removes a physical
chart, nor means that its density is zero.

All supported opens are retained. A resource-limited transition returns its
unchanged predecessor and exact request for caller-controlled retry. Invalid
owners remain errors. Immutable backward links share ancestry; the library
owns no process, thread or scheduling loop. This is in-memory progress, not
durable checkpointing or a measured allocator/RSS bound.

The actual `x^3+y^6` control follows all six next physical maps. One source
remains completed on all six; the active source transports through three empty
strict supports and eight nonempty target opens. Original factors, exceptional
births and Jacobians pass the existing independent terminal checks on every
physical chart. The `x^3+y^5` control checks source completion on all three
initial supported slots. Further controls cover foreign histories, wrong units,

The optional `(1+y^2)*(x^3+y^6)` first-center diagnostic reached its unchanged
600-second limit. It is retained as incomplete evidence outside the default
tests. No nonconstant-unit discovery or performance success is inferred from it.
Runtime-parameter first-center construction still refuses explicitly.
