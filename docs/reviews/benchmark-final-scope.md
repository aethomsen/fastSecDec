# Final benchmark campaign acceptance

The final user instruction drops no-contour double-box completion, makes
massive-kite no-contour conditional on prompt feasibility, and requests the
remaining report plus boundary and Landau-pinch discussions. This acceptance
closes that narrowed campaign. It does not declare the broader general
algebraic resolver complete.

The opening overview contains 66 measured timed sampler rows across eight
topologies. Every measured row has all requested generation, memory, artifact,
sector-count, sampling, normalized work/time error, absolute-weight ratio,
maximum-weight ratio and call-count columns. All uncertainties target epsilon
zero. QMC uses bounded sector residency; the ordinary discrete-MC scheduler is
the explicitly authorized exception. The physical double-box generation uses
the documented six-worker memory exception. Causal validation is off in timed
comparisons; mandatory precision handling remains enabled.

No-contour bubble, triangle, box, massless sunrise and massive sunrise have
complete numerical rows. Kite has complete contour rows but no complete
no-contour interval-density or full-atlas artifact. Its ten selected-boundary
charts and 40 precision checks do not change that classification. The two
no-contour double-box methods are omitted from the overview at user request;
the subsequent discussion retains 644,699 verified original cells and 307
admitted scalar charts of cell 0, with zero compiled kernels or estimates.
Illustrative full-atlas counts are explicitly neither forecasts nor bounds.

All owned double-box no-contour processes were stopped and confirmed dead.
The alternative-order solve exited -15 after 3,252.41 seconds on user
cancellation, with no survivors; the first-prefix campaign likewise exited
-15 after 427.71 seconds. Neither is a numerical failure or a completed solve.
The proposed saved-DTO merge experiment was never launched.

All twenty final dynamic-tuning jobs are closed. Independent exact-work QMC
controls recover fixed-like variance for the physical numerator and reduce it
for the scalar double box. The final native full-covariance comparison covers
234 complex blocks. Earlier campaign/reference checks and their roundoff or
high-variance limitations remain visible; no covariance regularization or
error inflation was used. Six capped MC work controls remain labeled
incomplete requested work. Square-root display corrections remain heuristics.

Accepted source milestones passed the scoped scientific suites, strict Clippy
and formatting recorded in their separate reviews. The last prefix-cover
milestone passed 32 normalized-map controls and 15 algebraic-evaluator controls.
The 37-page Typst report compiles without warnings. The coordinator visually
checked the updated double-box tables, boundary discussion and two Landau
pages; a data audit found no missing metric in any measured overview row.

Source, compact results, Typst and compiled PDF are published on
`no_deformation`. Raw outputs, private probes and unrelated user example files
remain untracked. No additional implementation or benchmark work is scheduled
after publication.
