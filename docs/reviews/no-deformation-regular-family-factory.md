# Automatic preparation of regular algebraic families

This increment connects the existing certified regular-family issuers to native
preparation and the CLI. It is a bounded path for complete fixed unit-cube
families with two or three cells, preserving the historical one-dimensional
rational path. It does not implement the general endpoint resolver, moving
bracket towers, parameter chambers or arbitrary cell inventories.

## Admission and native reuse

Discovery proposes rational cylinders through deterministic dyadic endpoint
queries. Native symGCAD domain certificates and their independent verification
establish endpoint signs over the original domain. The existing regular-section
and complete-family issuers then check selectors, derivatives, root gaps, cell
inventory and the transformed density. A proof sample's isolating interval is
never promoted to a runtime bracket.

The sextic control discovers two cylinders after 127 endpoint queries, with one
family admission attempt. Candidate misses are distinct from invalid proofs and
source errors. Query, degree, term, attempt and time limits give explicit
incomplete outcomes; they do not authorize missing cells or successful empty
artifacts. Native certificate calls can allocate internally, so these limits
are not hard allocator or aggregate-RSS guarantees.

Exact algebra, root refinement, symbolic derivatives, continuation and codecs
remain owned by Symbolica, Numerica and the existing threshold modules. The
factory adds bounded candidate selection and preparation wiring. It retains
factored/composed densities; only the small root polynomial is lowered into
shifted coefficients as described in the [arithmetic review](no-deformation-affine-root-brackets.md).

## Execution and ecosystem audit

The existing native preparation owner performs solve, verification and family
admission. Detached jobs retain local programs and compact metadata. The global
verified-family owner is released before compilation; weak-owner tests check
this lifetime. CLI children and the existing indexed writer handle both
generation schedules. No library-owned pool, estimator or alternate artifact
format is added.

Recovery restores raw evidence, verifies it again and compares the reconstructed
prepared identity. It does not treat saved verification metadata as authority.
The stored preparation extension binds coordinate roles and discovery settings;
absence of the extension preserves the old rational configuration. Original
dimensions, sampled dimensions, cell counts and chart counts are checked at
publication and inspection.

Saved selected-sector execution needs the native evaluator programs, not GCAD
or geometric re-preparation. The fresh default-native restoration controls
exercise this boundary. Caller-owned jobs, status/error boundaries and native
graph/parameter owners remain unchanged. No graph parser, algebra engine or
Python implementation is introduced. The earlier binding compile check covers
the preparation-setting extension. Existing rational/represented HEPKit paths
remain supported; the automatic native-cube regular-family HEPKit path needs
its separate end-to-end gate.

## Validation scope

The frozen preparation implementation passes 22 focused generation/recovery
tests, including fresh-process replay. The final arithmetic successor passes
14 maintained callback/codec groups and fresh default-native restoration. The
coherent candidate builds the CLI and passes strict workspace/all-target lint
and formatting. The coordinator verified all 34 source preimages/postimages in
patch `56180711e496dfc6faea9ebd19ac0fb89807c1b486b5063e1388f6fb526b3672`.
The independent factory interface/reuse review is pinned by
`a44a50ea6495da89059675bf506d98719db2d2458b086b9f9ef0f93f1ff81870`.
The root-arithmetic implementation received separate reviews; its author did
not count the factory review as independent review of their own arithmetic.

The actual numerical gate uses the sextic three-cell/four-chart family and all
four normal/serial generation–integration combinations. It checks the complete
complex Laurent vector and covariance, pole cancellation, full-integral scope,
artifact identity and the independent finite-imaginary reference
`3.8521160461762443`. For a fixed integration design, changing generation
scheduling must leave means and covariance identical. Ordinary shared-shift QMC
and serial independent-sector QMC have different covariance designs and are
compared statistically. All four lanes pass with 16,384 evaluations each, zero
failed or cutoff-zero samples, and an accuracy stop. Ordinary QMC gives finite
`-2.8173239218665542 + 3.852116046127196*i`; serial QMC gives
`-2.817323926777408 + 3.85211604197256*i`. Their respective real/imaginary
standard errors are `(5.58e-9, 2.91e-9)` and `(4.89e-9, 3.46e-9)`.
The pole components cancel within their reported uncertainties, and the entire
four-component vectors and 16 covariance entries are identical across
generation schedules for each integration mode.

These debug runs take about 27–28 seconds per lane. Between 10,482 and 10,511
points require arbitrary-precision rescue, reaching at most 256 bits. This is
correctness evidence, not a tuned sampling-performance claim. The existing
portable consumer suite passes all 88 tests. The consuming native workspace
passes 36 algebraic-kernel tests in 326.83 seconds, including complete-family
science and fresh-process restoration. Its three separately ignored helper
entries are invoked by parent tests. Strict workspace/all-target lint passes in
48.04 seconds; strict portable/all-target lint passes in 50.42 seconds.
Formatting and diff checks pass. Raw consuming logs remain untracked under
`target/no-deformation-factory-v3-joined/` and the corresponding portable logs.

Earlier arithmetic failures and their failed matrices remain preserved. A
successful small regular family cannot establish general algebraic resolution,
uniform parameter specialization, physical multiloop coverage or runtime
performance. Those obligations remain active in the approved plan.
