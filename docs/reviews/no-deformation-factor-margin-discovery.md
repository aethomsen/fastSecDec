# Positive-margin discovery for endpoint factors

The factor portfolio now tries a finite sequence of smaller exact rational margins when the initial candidate has no native certificate. This fixes four retained double-box U residuals whose positive lower bounds are smaller than the original trial margin. It changes candidate selection only: each accepted margin is positive, and symGCAD must certify and replay the same polynomial-minus-margin on the same domain. An exhausted search remains unknown.

The native API/source/probe audit found `certify_nonzero` and `verify_certificate`, but no public operation returning a positive lower bound; Bernstein coefficient construction and box transforms remain internal. FastSecDec therefore orchestrates at most twelve further margins, each divided by sixteen, and reuses native Rational arithmetic and certificates. It does not implement another positivity algorithm. Constant faces retain exact native Rational comparison. Every trial checks the existing rational-bit, cancellation and elapsed-time budget; a native miss followed by resource exhaustion remains an error rather than an unknown proof outcome.

The focused native probe preserved the actual expressions and domains. For all four U residuals, margins 2^-20, 2^-24 and 2^-28 failed to certify; 2^-32 produced a positive native certificate and passed exact replay. Those receipts do not discharge unrelated F residuals, whose tensor counts can exceed the owner's current explicit coefficient ceiling.

The maintained factor prefix passed all six groups, including the two new controls: a small positive polynomial with replay and changed-domain refusal; and exact zeros, invalid margins, rational-bit exhaustion, cancellation and a zero-variable constant. Both existing closed-scalar groups also passed. The feature-enabled joined libtest build completed without warnings; root and the runtime reviewer independently checked the source. Formatting and whitespace checks also pass for this focused milestone.

The 30-minute double-box diagnostic remains incomplete: 139 of 299 new descendants were attempted, 83 obtained complete factor/measure unit bundles, and 160 were not attempted. The eight earlier successful leaves are separate. Neither these certificates nor the margin search establish a common Laurent convergence strip, complete source-cell coverage, or a double-box integral. A nonzero polynomial or a missed trial margin is never treated as a closed-domain unit.
