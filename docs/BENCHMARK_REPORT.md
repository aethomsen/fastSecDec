# Benchmark report: source and editorial structure

The published document is [benchmark.pdf](benchmark.pdf). Its entry point is
[contour-deformation.typ](contour-deformation.typ); the historical filename is
retained so existing build commands and links continue to work.

## Build

```sh
nix-shell -p typst libertinus --run \
  'typst compile docs/contour-deformation.typ docs/benchmark.pdf'
```

Use the Libertinus Serif and Sans fonts for reproducible layout. The recorded
editorial build used Typst 0.15.1 and Libertinus 7.051. Where Nix does not expose
the font automatically, pass `--font-path` to its `share/fonts/opentype` directory.

## Reading order and ownership

The five landscape overview pages remain first, with no cover or contents page
before them. They use `benchmark-overview.typ` and the reviewed
`benchmark-overview-results.json`. The title, reading guide and linked contents
follow those tables. Six chapters then give a single current account:

| Chapter | Source | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Campaign design | `report-campaign.typ` | Metrics, clocks, inputs, settings and reproduction identities. |
| 2. Methods | `report-methods.typ` | Fixed and dynamic contours, branches, Jacobians and real-domain threshold decomposition. |
| 3. Findings | `report-results.typ` | Current scalar and double-box results, tuning, numerical compatibility and references. |
| 4. Implementation | `report-implementation.typ` | Native reuse, symbolic endpoints, Jacobian alternatives, residency and selected performance mechanisms. |
| 5. Support limits | `report-support-limits.typ` | Verified geometry versus closed charts versus executable sectors; unfinished kite and double-box cases. |
| 6. Critical loci | `report-critical-loci.typ` | Pinch diagnostics, exact scalar/crown probes, local resolution and prospective expansion by regions. |

Cross-references use Typst labels, not literal section/page numbers. Current
conclusions belong in these chapters. Do not append dated status updates or a
second title/introduction to the end of the report. Keep the difference between
measurements, correctness controls and unimplemented proposals explicit.

## Historical evidence retained outside the rendered report

The reorganization does not delete or reinterpret scientific measurements.
Older fragments remain as historical source companions to their immutable JSON
and review records; the current entry point no longer includes them:

- `benchmark-overview-details.typ`, `benchmark-compatibility.typ` and
  `double-box-dynamic-tuning.typ` supplied the current campaign chapters.
- `contour-scalar-results.typ`, `contour-cap-results.typ` and
  `contour-cap-double-box.typ` describe earlier resource/settings studies.
  Selected Jacobian and large-weight mechanisms remain in Chapter 4 with their
  original qualifications; their earlier rankings are not the current campaign.
- `no-contour-results.typ` and its table fragments retain the first bubble and
  high-degree polynomial controls. `no-contour-massive-sunrise-control.typ`
  retains the one-worker correctness/performance controls. Neither substitutes
  for fresh near-ten-minute comparison rows.
- `no-contour-gghh-feasibility.typ`, `no-contour-gghh-verified.typ`,
  `no-contour-gghh-density.typ` and `no-contour-boundaries.typ` record successive
  preparation stages. Chapter 5 consolidates their final scope; historical
  “pending” statements are not current support commitments.
- `landau-pinches.typ`, `gcad-critical-loci.typ` and
  `region-analysis-prospects.typ` supplied the connected research chapter.

The original five-minute/50-core physical campaign and the earlier long-form
entry point are also recoverable from commit `5bdded2`, with compact numerical
records still in this directory. No new measurements were generated by the
editorial revision.

## Editorial validation

Compile the report, inspect the affected pages and check heading destinations.
When changing prose only, compare both extracted text and rasterized images of
the first five pages against the preceding PDF. Confirm that the table source
and numerical JSON are unchanged, formulas and uncertainty caveats survive,
and historical settings are not silently attributed to current measurements.
See [the reorganization review](reviews/benchmark-report-reorganization.md).
